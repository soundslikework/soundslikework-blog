The Boss DS-1 is probably the most common pedal on any guitarist's board at some point in their life. It's cheap, it's orange, and it's been on pedalboards from Kurt Cobain to Steve Vai.

## The Sound

The DS-1 does one thing: hard clipping distortion with a lot of midrange. Crank the **Tone** knob clockwise and it gets harsh fast. Roll it back and it thickens up considerably. The **Distortion** knob goes from light breakup to full wall-of-fuzz saturation.

## Who It's For

If you're starting out and need distortion that works, the DS-1 is hard to argue with at its price point. It also responds well to mods (the Keeley Ultra mod being the most famous).

## Who Should Skip It

Players chasing smooth, vocal lead tones or warm blues grit will find the DS-1 brittle and aggressive. This pedal wants to be played loud.

## Verdict

An honest workhorse. Not the most refined distortion circuit out there, but wildly effective for the money. Every guitarist should try one at least once.
