## Why Less

Since I've started playing music, I've had this horrible urge for _more_. More gear, more time, more everything. A lot of people I know are more like accumulators, but I've kind of gone in cycles where I'll accumulate more things over a period of time, then sell a lot of it and start over. I'm more like waves.

Music always means a lot to me, but the gear sometimes means more or less. There are years where I hardly have time to play, and when that's happened, well, why keep so much stuff? So over time, things get sold, the collection gets pared down.
